## Lesson notes

### Пройдено

#### 1.1 — introduction

Практика:

- `My name is Maxim Ivanov.`
- `I'm from Russia.`
- `I work at Tinkoff Bank.`

Исправлено:

- `I'm work ...` → `I work ...`

#### 1.2 — questions with `to be`

Практика:

- `Are you from India?`
- `Are you okay?`
- `Are you a manager?`

Исправлено:

- `Is you okay?` → `Are you okay?`
- `okey` → `okay`

#### 1.3 — `he / she / they`, negatives

Практика:

- `He's from England.`
- `He's a developer.`
- `They are friends.`
- `He isn't tired.`
- `He is energetic.`
- `He is from Russia.`
- `He is my colleague.`

Исправлено:

- `He is so energy.` → `He is energetic.`
- `energy` — существительное
- `energetic` — прилагательное
